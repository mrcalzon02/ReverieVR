# Breakwater — late-campaign heavy armored assault transport

**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Authoritative repository:** `mrcalzon02/ReverieVR`, branch `main` only
**Status:** Native source implemented and GitHub write/readback verified; isolated genuine selection/penetration C++ suite passed; full native campaign/Android/Galaxy S9 device integration not yet executed.

## Requirement and implemented behavior

The user asked for a **separate heavy transport reserved for the really later levels, armored on the vessel itself**. The previous ordinary landing craft could carry one armored car, but its 85-HP ship hull was not armored. The heavy ship is a new `UnitType::ArmoredTransport` (added *after* existing enum values so original meanings stay stable) with **320 starting hull HP and actual per-weapon armor penetration**: 20-damage MG deals 4; 35-damage heavy machine gun deals 11; 42-damage flak deals 10; the 90-damage direct cannon and 125-damage missile keep their full damage. Artillery and airstrikes deal 75% of their nominal splash to this plated target; normal boats retain their old rules. Directly sinking it awards 210 credits, counts exactly one boat kill and prevents cargo magically deploying.

Spawn behavior is deterministic with the old seed and no new save fields or random runtime state. `invasion::SelectUnit` wraps the already-authored `waves::SelectInvasionUnit`, **preserving its early rowboat-only period, existing 4 pressure bands and increasingly threatening aircraft/gunboat/landing-craft mix**. Heavy transports begin only at progression grade >=10, in the latter half of the period, on seeded one-in-four outcomes. The first spawn of the very final wave is guaranteed to be a heavy flagship, ensuring this encounter cannot be skipped by unlucky RNG. Earlier grades retain exactly the existing `waves::SelectInvasionUnit` outputs.

A surviving heavy ship takes about 20 seconds to advance from z=-11 to shore at 0.44 units/sec (slower than 0.62 ordinary landing craft), and it **fires an onboard deck cannon** for 5 battery damage every 3.8 seconds in the real offshore gunboat firing zone, with its own event bit and the existing deeper armored-gun sound. The threat ETA code uses the actual 0.44 speed. At landfall it drops **two separately targetable 95-HP armored vehicles and four soldiers**. An initial integration check caught and fixed an accidental troop-cap clamp inherited from the single-car ordinary landing craft: the heavy gets all four, with rifle, left/right flanker and support-gunner tactics. Per-wave armor capacity increases from 10 to 20 (at most ten transport hulls x two vehicles each), while max live enemies, max infantry, existing wave count and original 80-byte shop checkpoint remain unchanged. The ordinary armored landing craft still holds just one car and up to three infantry.

The distinct VR model is game-authored solid vertex-color geometry, **not** a repurposed tiny boat image: angular large hull, plated sides and bow, command structure, raised onboard cannon, two visible vehicle cradles and physical landing ramp that lowers during the final approach. It is drawn in the active shared stereo game renderer, not just the old unused boat recipe. Its collision sphere is enlarged to 1.48, the existing target brackets to 1.07 half-width, and the compact center HUD identifies `HEAVY LANDER HP n/320`. The wreck system renders appropriately scaled fragments after an actual lethal hit. No new image licenses, samples, asset downloads, GitHub Actions, save ABI changes, controller-origin targeting bypasses or replacement music; the attributed loop remains `Mars des Souvenirs`.

## Follow-up implementation: heavy convoy composition and interruptible unloading (2026-10-16)
Live `main` reconciliation identified two concrete flaws: the heavy ship's modulo-four selection displaced gunboats in multiple pressure bands, while pressure band three's identical aircraft/gunboat modulo-three checks eliminated gunboats at high grades even without heavy ships. The patch preserves four-band escalation, changes only the highest-band collision's seeded gunboat roll mix, and then upgrades only normal surface landing craft to heavy ships using upper seeded bits. The late final-wave first heavy remains guaranteed.
Upon actual beaching, a heavy hull now stops at z=-2.2 for 1.25 seconds, still drawn with lowered ramp and targetable by direct fire, turrets, artillery and airstrikes. It ceases its offshore gun fire. Only after this hold survives do two real armored vehicles and four infantry deploy; destruction earlier denies the manifest. The native game issues one grounded water pulse/shoreline sound. Infantry insertion has a defensive array bound and reports only actual spawned troops. No platform ABI, native Home UI, new third-party assets or shop-save fields were added, and Mars des Souvenirs remains preserved.
The existing unit and campaign test sources were extended for preserving gunboats/aircraft, four-band mixtures, heavy threshold/flagship and ramp hold. Full native C++ campaign execution, Android APK and Galaxy S9 device validation still need actual run evidence.
