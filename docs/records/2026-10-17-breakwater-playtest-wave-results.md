# Breakwater Battery playtest repairs: waves, audio, controls and debrief

**Date:** 2026-10-17 (Alaska). **Source:** `main`; runtime / Galaxy S9 headset acceptance pending.

The installed phone build was reported to have missing battle sound and music, very few hostile craft despite large ammunition magazines, no practical in-combat weapon selection, a cannon overlapping its own battery parapet, apparent enemy-on-enemy gunfire, a visible void outside the Iron Quay playing lane, and no explicit save selection or fresh-campaign restart. A later playtest request requires a score report and short fanfare between waves, with choice to return to the upgrades shop or start the next wave.

**Audio:** Android `VrActivity.onNativeModuleSoundCueRequested` formerly rejected sound IDs 16–21 even though these were valid Breakwater events; it now passes the full 1–27 native range. The Android game sound bank retries missing `AudioTrack` voices after output-route initialization and at bounded later intervals, logs failures and increases SFX gain. Hosted Breakwater keeps the existing synthetic coastal surf ambience enabled in addition to its owner-authorized `Mars des Souvenirs` theme; a missing hosted cue ID no longer turns off music completely. A wave-clear produces a new 1.18-second G4–B4–D5–G5 musical fanfare, not an unlicensed file. Exact acoustic acceptance remains a physical test.

**Combat:** Per-wave total craft changes from `min(10, 2+tier+location+day-1+night)` to `min(30, 3*(2+tier+location+day-1+night))`, giving opening counts of 6/9/12/15. Spawn spacing becomes 0.65 seconds rather than 1.6 seconds. At most 12 live ships coexist; dead infantry and destroyed armored vehicle slots are reused for later landings without displacing active threats. Live soldier/vehicle capacity remains bounded. This extends sustained encounters rather than introducing an unbounded spawn pool.

**Controls and physics:** A dedicated `native-breakwater` input profile routes Daydream touchpad X into the native move-X action. Deliberate left/right deflections cycle **owned** guns; centering the pad rearms selection. Shared cannon muzzle and mesh now mount at y=-0.53 instead of y=-0.76, and the traverse prohibits depression beyond the firing line. Offshore gunboat tracer geometry is shortened so it does not visually pass through other enemy boats on the way to the battery; hostile combat effects still apply to the battery, not their fleet. Iron Quay now extends toward the lateral view with scenic harbor piers, posts and a gantry; the other beaches gain wider flank terrain.

**Save and progression:** The old `breakwater-v1.bin` file is no longer read. Three isolated `breakwater-run-[1..3]-v2.bin` slots are independently scanned, chosen on entry, and persisted using the unchanged validated 80-byte campaign payload. The shop offers an explicit double-select **New Run / Confirm Reset** button scoped only to the chosen slot; old or other-slot content is not deleted.

**Wave-end experience:** The authoritative campaign continues to settle its normal rewards and transition to Shop. The native presentation holds a temporary results intermission with vessels sunk, aircraft destroyed, armor destroyed, infantry stopped/landed, total credits gained, hull lost and remaining totals. The first press after firing is required to be a new edge, not an inherited held trigger. **UPGRADES** dismisses the results and reveals the shop; **NEXT WAVE** checkpoints the shop then launches the next assault. Shop replay and old pre-v2 saves cannot accidentally skip the results screen on that same session. Fanfare triggers from the real wave-clear event, not a countdown.

Native scripts/test source and Android/NDK build should be run and reviewed before claiming a releasable APK. Stereo view bounds, controller touchpad integration, sound coexistence, later-wave difficulty and subjective detail/readability still require Galaxy S9 playtesting. All other modules and hosted ABI are unchanged.
