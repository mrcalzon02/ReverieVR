# Red Ledger native stock assets — second original supplier

The original Unreal 5.7 exports remain in their full source resolution. Eight recovered SKUs, covering supplier 101 and UED supplier 202, are mapped to their true FBX visible mesh identities and original 1024px PNG textures. Offer numbers follow the existing saved catalog and do not affect save compatibility.

The GLES2 renderer places merchandise on four physical rack levels, with a dedicated column for each supplier; selection still places the chosen original model on the stockroom podium or on the bar counter. Shelf copies are only visible while serialized inventory has units on hand. No source art is fabricated for unimported SKUs.

Android VR GPU textures are uploaded from deterministic 512px 2×2 downsampling of the 1024px original pixel data. This reduces the merchandise texture VRAM from 4 MiB to 1 MiB per SKU without modifying source files. The native original-asset test exercises the exact eight mesh paths, geometry decoding, PNGs, negative cases, stock quantities, and downsampling.

The work is source integration, not yet a verified Galaxy S9 hardware performance or visual capture. Unimported suppliers 303–606 and non-merchandise assets remain pending.
