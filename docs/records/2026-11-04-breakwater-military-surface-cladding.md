# Breakwater military fortification materials — 2026-11-04

Context: Breakwater is the coastal-defense battery hosted in ReverieVR. It is not a lighthouse, fishing village or maritime exploration application. This pass uses the already implemented gun emplacement geometry, campaign locations and combat camera, rather than introducing an unrelated level or concept art.

Implementation: two new deterministic 64×64 RGBA military textures (woven sandbag/jute and chipped olive enamel) join the original shingle, quay concrete, basalt and oxidized steel in one 128×448 game-private atlas (224 KiB RGBA8 per GL context). Preserve the existing 128×256 combat sprite coordinates. materials::AppendBoxCladding covers the six real faces of selected existing defense geometry with an offset 3 mm outward from their original flat-color backup, in the same sprite VBO and draw pass: bunker deck, bunker cheeks/sidewalls/bulkheads, pillboxes, 12 sandbag segments, equipment lockers, rear steel door, and six beach stakes. Material selection is authored by the existing emplacement geometry emitter, not guessed by mesh dimensions. The unchanged legacy four-argument emitter still builds the same flat geometry for existing tests and consumers.

No new target IDs, physics colliders, projectile interception, save schema, game controls or weapon movement. Emplacement geometry is unchanged, so the player's gun-sight corridor, projectile origin and infantry landing route remain the source of truth. These surfaces are game-private material dressing and must not be promoted to shared SDK prior to S9/GLES validation.

Validation: the exact material header blob (Git SHA 7cf4aaa02962438d9e4286f1fbe8c517adea7a0d) and material regression test (Git SHA 63ba0bd6dcb8bb3661950ff1099123b350d1d72e) were compiled and executed locally on GCC/Clang with C++17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror. The test asserts determinism, all six tile pixel counts, finite UV coordinates, 12-triangle box surfaces, and invalid-dimension rejection. The emplacement host test was extended for 12 materialized sandbags, metalwork and finite material geometries, but the complete test is not executed in this environment. Android NDK, complete native module, stereo screenshots, real Galaxy S9 and APK publication are also unverified.

Risks: six faces per box can add material overdraw; 64px material tiles may stretch on large bunker walls. After installed headset captures, review visual tiling, nighttime consistency, depth occlusion, maintain 60 Hz stereoscopy and test sustained temperature before acceptance. There are no extra draw passes, GL resources or new package dependencies.
