## Actual menu screenshot archive (2026-10-29)

The new `.github/workflows/native-game-menu-archive.yml` workflow uses the *installed Android 10 x86_64 ReverieVR hub*, calls the same JNI/GLES2 menu-capture test used by hub acquisition integration, and exports a screenshot and measured framebuffer CRC for **each of the 15 native modules** (seven featured games and eight Classics). It is not an art generator, concept screenshot, or registry-only simulation.

`scripts/ci/promote_native_game_gallery.py` refuses to publish until the complete captured-IDs set, 512×512 PNG integrity, nonblank content, and per-game CSV evidence pass. A successful run commits all 15 untouched source screenshots to `art/archive/native-game-menus/<game-id>.png`, all 15 derived title-card JPEGs to `art/archive/native-game-menus/cards/<game-id>.jpg`, their SHA-256/CRC manifest, and the 672×920 four-column `app/src/main/assets/native/gallery/menu-preview-atlas.jpg` actually consumed by the Home gallery. Cards retain the entire menu framebuffer (letterbox if needed; no stretch/crop). IDs and positions are explicit in `NativeGamesGallery.java` and checked in `NativeGamesGalleryTest.java`.

The original seven-title 336×230 mood-board/reference atlas remains a **temporary compatibility fallback only** until a verified complete native capture is committed. A failed or unavailable game never receives reference art disguised as a runtime capture. Prior hub-acquisition testing on 2026-10-29 captured 14/15: Procedural Test Chamber failed with `incomplete scene`. Its authored scene was missing a required trailing newline, repaired on `main` in commit `eb51df72b336d9e70e79980747801009c8d9b265`. Game-screenshot publication is only complete when the new workflow succeeds and the generated atlas and archive paths can be read back from `main`. Desktop Java Swing ports are not substituted for these Android screenshots.
