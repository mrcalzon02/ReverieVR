# Breakwater coastal rebuild: first source implementation

Status: implemented and locally exercised in the actual GLES module; NOT an APK release or physical-headset acceptance. This is the first rebuild increment, not the completion of the full overhaul.

## Intent and baseline

The owner approved rebuilding the coastal defense experience, retaining the working campaign framework, with fixed-position visibility, controlled texture density and distance LOD. Starting main: `c373bceaaff1c69bac71bc9eaf2575a57e00688f`. Its combat beach occupied 7.72 by 1.94 source units, ocean stopped at -110 and the host far plane was 240. Merely increasing the far clip would retain the miniature combat space.

## Executed

- `breakwater_battlefield.h` separates deterministic campaign/save coordinates from physical placement. Lateral positions expand 18x and approach positions 55x; models retain independently appropriate dimensions (infantry 2.4x, armor/large craft 3–4x), rather than becoming 55x giants. Each placement supplies the inverse ray transform used by authoritative damage selection, matching the actual rendered mesh. Projectile/cover helpers retain their old 40-unit defaults for existing callers and accept the game's bounded 2400m range explicitly.
- The battery floor is approximately 19m above mean water. A connected triangle-interpolated shoreline slopes from the bunker to a waterline about 114m away and continues underwater. Terrain sampling uses the exact mesh triangles, not an unrelated collision polynomial. Immutable grid heights are baked once and shared by grounding and targeting. Heavy hull offsets keep their bow/ramp near the common landing line instead of multiplying the old center offset into a 66m gap; complete embark/disembark animation remains open.
- `breakwater_coastal_world.h` emits only upper ground surfaces, skipping deeply submerged cells. Its shared nonuniform grid has 2–4m near cells, 8–32m middle cells and coarse untextured background cells. One 64px authored tile per near cell gives approximately 16–32 texels/m locally, deliberately decreasing with distance. Distant land uses vertex colors rather than dense tiled art. All three legal defense viewpoints and head rotation remain supported, so geometry visible from another station is retained.
- Static coastline geometry is cached by theater/day-night and uploaded to separate static VBOs once per change/context. Dynamic water, units and equipment do not trigger static coastline rebuild/upload. The existing optional pre-eye callback prepares shared CPU scene data; older hosts retain the draw fallback. Both eyes observe one simulation state.
- Animated low-poly water extends about 3.4km offshore. Distant physical hills are about 1.6–2km away; the packaged four panorama skies remain a separate background. Both integrated ReverieVR and the retained optional companion use a 6000m far plane. This is a native low-cost water implementation, not a claim that every shared Sitka/editor water/weather feature has been integrated.
- Full, reduced and distant 3D unit tiers use station-relative distance. Far hulls remain volumetric eight-triangle forms, not sprites. Near owner rowboats/APCs and all five available imported models remain in use. New target tests exercise a real 500m+ hull intersection; initial campaign spawns are around 600m, not yet the planned 1–3km starts.
- Cannon mouth, trajectory guide, hit selection, impacts and ground/water misses now share metre-space positions and scaled drop coefficients. Terrain and destructible cover stop shots. Decorative bunker/dressing meshes still lack comprehensive collision; do not describe this as complete fortification collision.
- Combat readouts/support/backup weapon controls move to a station-anchored physical instrument case on the right, facing the operator, leaving the forward view clear. All glyphs, icons and picking use the same forward/inverse transform. The left rack also faces the operator. Its old deep cuboids selected neighboring shelves before the aimed shelf; plane-based shelf selection fixes the existing regression. Neither interface follows headset yaw.
- The machine gun starts a single-barrel, belt-fed receiver/shroud/grip pass, removing its four outboard barrel tubes. The missile launcher no longer contains the common central gun tube. Remaining heavy/flak/cannon shapes, their common carriage, authored weapon UVs and modular upgrades still need the next modeling pass.

## Observed validation

- `scripts/verify-breakwater-visuals.sh`: PASS for all available GCC cases, including the new battlefield tests, actual 16 authored PNG decode/atlas fallback, original geometry, five weapon shapes, rack picking, defense points, trajectory, schedules and asset scope. Clang is unavailable locally and explicitly skipped.
- Complete `breakwater_campaign_test.cpp` linked against the real campaign implementation: PASS. Campaign state/save layout was not changed.
- New battlefield tests: PASS for all four theaters, finite/bounded coast and water geometry, actual triangle grounding, scaled cover AABB hits, normalized long-distance hull hits from all three stations, ray misses and first terrain/water crossings.
- `scripts/verify-breakwater-world-gl.sh`: PASS on real Mesa OpenGL ES (software driver). It compiles/includes the actual game module and campaign, loads 16/16 materials, 4/4 skies and 5/5 imported models, renders both eyes at all three stations, checks immutable static geometry, console render/pick parity, simulation immutability during draw, and destroys/recreates the EGL context before rendering again. Its displayed mixed fleet is an explicitly controlled test fixture, not a naturally reached or installed-APK campaign claim.
- Native ABI contract executable: PASS. No ABI structure changed; Breakwater now supplies the existing `prepare_frame` tail.
- Native doc guard initially crashed because three mapped-path constants were undefined. Those definitions are restored without disabling checks. The guard now runs and reports pre-existing Test Chamber shader-helper drift (`ReverieNativeCompileShader`); that separate game's source was not changed to silence it. This broader diagnostic remains open.
- No Android SDK/NDK, emulator or reference phone is available locally. No APK build, install, publication, physical S9 stereo/comfort/frame-pacing/thermal acceptance is claimed by these local checks. Mesa timings are not phone performance evidence.
