# OMEGA FRONT — World Bible
**Project:** RV-GAME-06, native ReverieVR tactical strategy RPG  
**Title:** Omega Front  
**Status:** Creator-provided setting canon plus explicitly provisional expansion; NO GAME IMPLEMENTATION CLAIMED  
**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Mechanics design charter:** [WAR_WITHOUT_END.md](WAR_WITHOUT_END.md), retained at its original filename to preserve links from the initial working-title phase.

## 1. Creator-established canon (authoritative)

In the late twentieth century, the **Ouroboros Corporation** broke through a frontier of **technomantic research**. Its work was followed by the rupture of space and time; nobody knows precisely how or why the disaster occurred. **The planet itself was torn in half.** Wounds in reality appeared across what remained of the world.

From these spatial and temporal rifts came innumerable beings and societies, some monstrous, some recognizably human, some from the past, some from the future. Ancient Roman soldiers might fight techno-abominations of the remote future on the same ground. These arrivals include populations utterly unable to understand baseline modern humans, and desperate refugee remnants driven out of their rightful time. This is not one orderly alternate history.

Some parts of the surviving world remain livable, even idyllic, for humans who are **unprotected, unshielded and unaugmented**. These territories are called **Eden Zones**. They are the stakes of the principal human wars: places where people can breathe, live and raise communities, and therefore prizes that corporations and organizations will kill to control. Outside them are the **wastelands**, the **barren zones** and the **anomaly fields**.

**Seventeen factions** compete over what remains. Their motives, capabilities and origins vary widely. Some autonomous swarms consume resources in their path. Some groups are only trying to secure refuge; others embody ancient worlds, futuristic war-making or unfamiliar forms of life. Rivalry for sovereignty and breathable ground drives the setting; there is no requirement that all seventeen factions be irredeemably hostile.

The player's role: **establish a base, hold territory, defend the right to exist, expand into wastelands, fight in anomalies and contend with every power that covets the player's land.** Start as a vulnerable foothold. Build an army, industry, knowledge and the means to shape what remains of the broken planet.

### Canon locks and unresolved truths

**Locked:** Ouroboros Corporation; late-20th-century technomantic breakthrough; simultaneous planetary bisection and reality breaches; portals carrying past, future and monstrosities; Eden Zones specifically habitable by unprotected baseline humans; the three additional geographic terms; exactly seventeen competing factions; autonomous consuming swarms and dislocated refugees among them; and base-building territorial survival warfare.

**Not yet specified:** what Ouroboros intended or actually did; physical cause of the split; how the bisected planet remains habitable; whether its halves can be seen from one another; what happened to Ouroboros; Earth versus another world-name; geographic/topological layout; present campaign date; exact faction names/affiliations; details of portal mechanics; whether portals link one or many timelines; player faction identity; and whether the ten prestige cycles result from the same mechanism as the Rupture.

**Do not close unresolved mysteries by implication.** Later design must label explanations proposed until the creator accepts them.

## 2. Proposed opening narration (new draft; not additional locked canon)

> **WELCOME TO THE OMEGA FRONT.**
>
> In the closing years of the twentieth century, Ouroboros Corporation reached into the machinery beneath reality. Something reached back.
>
> The world split. Not its nations, not its people: **the planet itself**. Wounds appeared across what remained. Armies marched out of centuries long buried. Men bearing bronze shields fought machines built in centuries that may never have happened. Others came through with no language anyone could recognize, driven by hunger, fear, duty or instruction.
>
> A few places were spared. Green valleys. Clear water. Air you can breathe without a filter. We call them **Eden Zones**. The name sounds like paradise until you count how many people will kill to stand on the grass.
>
> Beyond them lie the wastelands, the barren territories and the anomaly fields, where geography and causality are considered optional. Seventeen powers have marked the ruins as theirs. Corporations, armies, refugees, kings and swarms all have their reasons.
>
> You have a little land, a small force and no guarantee that either survives until morning. Build your base. Arm your people. Hold your ground. Push past the walls. The world will not give you room to exist.
>
> **Take it.**

The narrative tone is severe, bleak, occasionally dry, but the text above is **proposed** presentation copy, not an additional historical source.

## 3. Regions become distinct strategic systems (PROPOSED)

| Region | Established interpretation | Proposed campaign function | Proposed battlefield consequences |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eden Zone** | Naturally habitable for baseline unshielded, unaugmented humans | Settlements, food, water, hospitals, legitimacy, civilians, highest territorial stakes | Sieges, infrastructure protection, evacuation, high strategic density, faction politics |
| **Wasteland** | Ruined land beyond safe habitation | Salvage, roads, devastated factories, contested expansion front | Degraded supply routes, contaminated ground, roaming raids, ambush terrain |
| **Barren Zone** | Extremely hostile, generally unlivable land | Exotic mining, shortcuts, isolated relics, rare materials | Life-support requirements, equipment wear, fuel and transport attrition |
| **Anomaly Field** | Territory changed by spatial/temporal ruptures | Technomantic study, temporal materials, deep progression, dangerous breakthroughs | Chronal effects, altered terrain laws, rift reinforcements, unstable battle conditions |

**Proposed map model:** each sector has a primary land class (Eden, Wasteland or Barren) plus optional anomaly-field overlays. This allows an Eden Zone to become anomalous; it does not assert definitively that canon anomaly fields overlap other environments. Label world-map hazards honestly. Avoid unavoidable lethal effects unseen in reconnaissance.

A **captured Eden Zone should not merely be a currency faucet**. Track baseline-human habitability, water, agricultural yield, local population, hospitals, power, civilian trust, infrastructure damage and evacuation capacity. Conquest through indiscriminate bombardment may win the tile and destroy what made it worth taking. Restoring it requires real engineering, supplies and governance.

Wastelands make salvage and convoy protection essential. Barren zones test advanced logistics instead of only inflating enemy health. Anomaly fields demand reconnaissance, equipment preparation and specialized doctrines.

**The split planet must be depicted as actually physically broken.** What the separation looks like and how its atmosphere, gravity, oceans and horizon work are open art-and-lore decisions. No speculative model is canon yet.

## 4. Era collision in combat (PROPOSED)

Temporal displacement creates unique societies and military economies, not palette-swapped unit tiers. Every force has a time of origin, culture, command hierarchy, recruitment method, technological compatibility, infrastructure dependence, resource needs and attitude toward baseline humans.

- **Technology retains physical meaning:** a bronze spear will not penetrate intact futuristic armor because levels demand parity. Older forces can win through logistics raids, fortifications, exposed personnel, terrain, alliances, sabotage, theft and learned adaptation.
- **Superiority imposes costs:** future armies depend on rare components, power sources, maintenance skills and replacement parts that may not exist in the present. Their equipment can be terrifying and operationally fragile.
- **Refugees are not automatic villains:** soldiers from a shattered past may need shelter, food and a defensible place to live. The player may negotiate, absorb, exploit or destroy them, with lasting political consequences.
- **Swarms consume a real world:** robotic and biological autonomous swarms grow by processing terrain, loot, wreckage, infrastructure or biomass. Their replication costs material and creates interruptible supply pathways.
- **Anomaly effects must be legible:** initiative reversals, altered heat flow, delayed shots, unstable cover and chronal environmental hazards affect simulation rules through inspectable, reproducible modifiers. Avoid arbitrary script kills.
- **Doctrine matters:** a future army might be perfect at coordinated ranged combat but vulnerable to signal corruption. An ancient formation can excel at cohesion and engineering yet be almost helpless without allies against air superiority.

Battle units need faction + era identity + class + equipment + doctrine + command network + logistics, rather than assigning equal strength to units solely because they share a level number.

## 6. Campaign beginning and world pressure (PROPOSED)

The player's faction starts at the edge of a small Eden foothold with a few defenders, water and power infrastructure, limited ammunition and salvage, and no convincing claim to immunity. Nearby factions are competing for the same local resources.

**Suggested initial operations:**
1. Defend a habitat and water system from a raid. Civilian loss and resource expenditure matter.
2. Push into the wasteland to recover fabrication components, then bring them home along an actual supply route.
3. Encounter displaced fighters from another century. Recon determines their origin and objectives; battle is not automatically inevitable.
4. Survive a deliberate assault from a territorial organization that covets the Eden foothold. Build and defend field works.
5. Perform the first dangerous expedition into an anomaly pocket. Discover something that eventually connects to equipment inspection and world progression.

The player faction, its ethics, organizational form and commanders are *undecided*, not assumed to be Ouroboros or a heroic remnant nation.

## 7. Progression, inspections and world prestiges in this setting (PROPOSED)

The companion gameplay charter requires commanders, soldiers, formations, weapons, armor, accessories, base, world and doctrine each to level independently, with deliberately vast battle-earned advancement and dangerous, explorable nested inspection rooms.

Proposed diegetic explanation: progressive technomantic understanding makes a **formation's command structure, a weapon's temporal imprint, an armored component's internal structure, or even an Eden facility's systems** accessible as physically traversable pocket-realities. Inspectors must fight to stabilize and improve the system. This is a **gameplay hypothesis**, not a confirmed explanation for the Ouroboros disaster.

The 10 complete level-100 **World Reclamations** can be presented as increasingly difficult reconstructions of the broken world. Each might reveal a different facet of the disaster and unlock another piece of the true-ending path. **It remains deliberately unanswered** whether these reconstruct one timeline, move the player between alternate worlds, or reflect something stranger.

The player must not get free progress from a calendar timer. Dangerous tactical battle and costly victories remain the source of growth. As the world and armies advance, large late-game numbers must coexist with sensible material/equipment interactions; the ancient bronze fighter can become an ingenious veteran without effortlessly ignoring the penetrative properties of advanced armor.

## 8. Visual, audio, narrative and UI language

**Visuals:** chibi/chip-mini sprites, original designs, 4–8 facing silhouettes, small atlases, crisp tactical tiles and restrained early-PlayStation-era structure geometry. The world is horrifying in subject matter despite its miniature presentation. Ancient infantry and future machines must be instantly distinct in shape and equipment; no copyrighted visual lifts from the inspirations.

**World palette cues:** Eden greenery/water/settlements; wasteland burnt ruins and industrial scrap; barren scoured mineral ground; anomalies impossible tile patterns and repeated spatial landmarks. Do not rely exclusively on color to communicate terrain hazards, faction identity or statuses.

**Audio:** retro chiptune plus military percussion, faction-specific command sounds, mechanical weapon effects and chronal distortions without unlicensed samples. Severe danger gets readable visual cues too.

**Tone:** brutal survival, territorial ambition, displacement and the tragedy of people forced into wars from eras they never chose. The dry humor belongs to the absurdity of the systems, not to mocking refugees or loss.

**UI:** dense, scrollable faction/sector/units rosters, minimal explanation around self-evident buttons, clearly separated canon intelligence vs discovered intelligence, and compact combat reports.

## 9. Data and test interfaces for later implementation

- **World:** rupture marker, world seed, current cycle, fractured-topology ID, laws active, discovered histories.
- **Sector:** stable ID, primary land class, anomaly overlays, adjacency, territory controller, habitability, population, supply, environmental hazard, infrastructure state.
- **Faction[17]:** stable ID, original time/culture, resources, doctrine, AI policy, player relation, controlled sectors, refugee policy, survival goals.
- **Rift:** entrance and exit properties only when discovered, stability and hazard rules, arrivals, effects, research evidence.
- **Battle:** reproducible terrain and era modifiers, seed, participating squads, ammo/fuel/wounds, casualties, action log, objectives, after-action influence.
- **Covenant:** exact XP-track and level-100 proofs, ten world prestige records, inherited options, law seals and ultimate ending gates.

Tests need deterministic seeds, valid faction IDs, sector connectivity, correct civilian habitat mathematics, honest AI fog constraints, softlock-free rift/sector exits, no double-awarded XP, and valid ten-prestige true-ending gate.
