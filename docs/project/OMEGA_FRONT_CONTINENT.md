# Omega Front — Continental Geography (first implemented world slice)

**Scope:** World topology and real terrain-texture strategic display, distinct from the existing 24x24 tactical encounter grid and sixteen-sector saved Hearthfall campaign. This is a first world-construction step, **not a claim of 600 playable settlements**.

The world is a deterministic **30 × 20 = 600-cell continental geography**, generated from a persistent campaign seed. Its coastline, gulf, cape, mountains, northern snow, estuary, river, forests, farmland, ruins, eastern badlands, and trade roads are rendered from the existing approved Omega Front terrain tiles, now including salt flats, gravel, steppe, pine floor, frost tundra, orchard, scree, riverbank, shallows/fords, rubble, rusted wasteland, and roads (with flat sea/terrain fallback). **Quarantined** tiles remain excluded. Strategic scale uses the real game asset loader and separate one-eye draw calls. The sixteen canonical playable Hearthfall sectors are located in a permanent 4x4 district within that continent. Sector IDs, operations, intelligence levels, campaign checkpoints, and serialized 2048-byte v9 saves remain untouched. **Marching economy is affected**: travel into badlands/highlands demands two rations rather than one, while existing diplomatic transit passes waive the terrain cost; movement still permits under-supplied withdrawal with the established trust penalty per missing ration. There is no arbitrary teleport into unimplemented provinces.

The default strategic presentation opens at **CONTINENT** scale. A clearly labeled **REGION** control enters the already-operational local campaign map, where all existing movement, recon, diplomacy and tactical commands remain functional; **WORLD** returns to the large map. Main-menu, tactical battles, paused saves, and restored games retain their existing controls. The 600 visible cells describe stable geography and future theater positions, while only the marked sixteen-sector Hearthfall district currently supports gameplay. The campaign already features a real broken viaduct and cross-border claim/supply consequences; the new deterministic terrain cost adds a direct logistical consequence without inventing populated territory. Unknown faction/control intel remains hidden.

The continental generator is pure C++17: `omega_front_continent.h`. `omega_front_continent_test.cpp` verifies 600 cells, seed stability/difference, multiple terrain classes, land/sea ratios, the complete connected sixteen-sector district and stable sector-ID mapping. It has no Android, GLES or external library dependency. `omega_front_module.cpp` consumes the generator using the already loaded, licensed and approved terrain sprites—no placeholder pseudo-text, unexplained asset licenses, or unreviewed art imports.

**Immediate continuation:** Make the rest of the continent navigable and economically functional (regional resources, settlements, factions, persistent fog-of-war, roads and strategic consequences), evolving the campaign save format with a rigorous migration from v1–v9. Derive each tactical battlefield from the actual province terrain; confirm seams, rivers, coastlines, and road connections in native render tests. Install and run the APK on an S9, measure sustained frame pacing, and do not claim full world completion before those tests. The continent's present 600 landscape cells are **not** a 600-sector operational campaign; those are deliberately distinct acceptance gates.

## Tactical pacing correction (same development iteration)

The tactical battlefield remains 24×24 tiles, and its **simulation is turn-based**. The old quarter-second loop in `omega_front_controls.h` is removed; neither update `delta_seconds`, idle time, nor stereo rendering advances enemies, allies, ammunition, mission progress, or siege deadlines. Input queues friendly orders at any pace. The **END TURN** button at the top of the battlefield and the **END TURN / RESOLVE** command in the tactical **MODES** menu trigger exactly one `Battle::Step()` (friendly actions, enemy response, objective evaluation). Holding the button cannot accidentally resolve successive rounds. The existing `paused` checkpoint field is preserved as a legacy hold gate, and old checkpoint formats remain readable. A resolved turn is saved immediately, not after a sixteen-tick timer threshold. Tests cover indefinite idle, explicit turn advancement, held-button suppression, tactical menu dispatch, save/load consistency and ended-battle reports.

**Known limitation:** Tactical tiles still use the authored 24×24 encounter layouts; continent-derived battlefield map variation and continent-wide political simulation have not yet been built. The 600 cells are **real displayed geography with a local supply cost**, not 600 interactive political provinces. This is a foundational continent pass, not completion of the world-campaign feature.
