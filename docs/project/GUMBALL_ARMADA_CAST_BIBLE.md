# Gumball Armada — Supporting Cast and Encounter Design Bible

**Status:** AUTHORING / PLANNED. This document records approved character concepts and proposed future encounter mechanics; it is **not implementation evidence**. No associated portraits, character models, sprites, dialogue recordings, authored encounter assets, shop UIs or companion systems are shipped by virtue of this document.

**Game:** *Gumball Armada: The Search for the Candy King* in ReverieVR. **Scope:** six shop personalities and operators, two potential friendly mid-sortie arrivals, and six recurring enemy characters (including the Biff and Baff pair). All are original candy-space characters. They coexist with the missing Candy King, Gubernatorial Gumball, Sally Scrunch, Captain Cringe, Pete Pucker and Senator Sauer.

**Production mapping:** S05/S17 (art, effects, recordings, dialogue), S08 (shops, acquisition and inventory), S09 (unlocks/quests), S10–S11 (places and event composition), S12–S16 (regional story arcs). Refer to [the staged plan](GUMBALL_ARMADA_STAGED_PLAN.md), [content matrix](GUMBALL_ARMADA_CONTENT_MATRIX.md) and [current status](GUMBALL_ARMADA.md).

## 1. Cast-wide direction and contracts

The supporting cast should give the player a reason to **return to inhabited ports**. Each regular has a clear silhouette, an immediately recognizable speaking cadence, a distinct specialization and at least one relationship with another recurring character. They are residents of a threatened candy civilization, not interchangeable smiling sales icons.

Keep character appearances compact: a short portrait/radio panel or a recognizable little support/enemy craft should work in VR without blocking the flight reticle. Route/shop screens must be information-dense rather than opening huge dedicated tabs for each character. People can be amusing without becoming useless comic relief. Characters remember meaningful help, completed contracts, and serious mistakes where the game actually records those facts.

**Existing vendor taxonomy remains five archetypes:** Royal Outfitters, Scrap Bazaar, Citric Black Market, Mobile Trader, Faction Quartermaster. The six personalities below are *operators and specialists*, not six new universal economy systems. Two can occupy specialist counters within one vendor archetype. Store rules, inventory ownership, price validation and faction consequences remain governed by S08; no infinite cash, free duplicate parts, or cosmetic-only part listings.

**Reference controls:** Galaxy S9/Daydream is seated 3DoF with a clickable directional touchpad; Home/Menu are shell-reserved. Companion calls, extra lives, bombs and countermeasures must use contextual game actions, automatic bounded events, or future accessible remappable controls—never fictitious buttons. Optional allies should never require online services, purchases of real-world currency, or an intrusive overlay. Store and encounter appearance must be stable under reload/seed where appropriate.

**Availability:** all entries below are currently *planned*, including their supposed bonuses and equipment. The existing three-gun combat and starter modular shipyard are not evidence that vendors sell those parts or that named companions/enemies are implemented.

## 2. Shopkeepers and station operators

### CAST-TILDA — Tilda Taffy
**Venue:** **Taffy Trawl Hullworks**. **Economy fit:** Royal Outfitters' heavy-hull and structural repair specialist (not a separate sixth economy class). **Story position:** early accessible ally and shipbuilding mentor.

**Personality:** broad-shouldered, practical, motherly and kindly blunt; never praises a dangerous build just to flatter its pilot. Keeps an endless strand of pink taffy between her teeth while assessing how a vessel survived. She calls broken machines "poor babies" and treats hopeless-looking first ships with genuine affection.

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** rolled industrial sleeves, sugar-glass welding visor, broad tool apron, permanent dockyard smudges, thick taffy coil; warm pinks and forged caramel browns. Strong distinctive outline even as a small bust portrait.

**Planned systems:** frames, armor, bulkheads, hull repairs, mass distribution, stabilization, repair contracts and eventually emergency patch services. Her dialogue can identify overloaded/offline ship systems truthfully from current design statistics, rather than presenting a fake diagnosis. S08 merchant rules apply to every stock transaction.

**Character hooks:** mentors the commander after the first costly loss; openly worries about Pip's reckless flying; distrusts Fizzlebloom's experimental overcharge tuning. Possible optional job: recover a missing shipment of licorice trusses, then unlock reinforced frames.

**Voice and sample lines:** warm, low, dry with working-class authority. "Sugar, this ship's held together by optimism and caramel string." / "Don't pout. I've rebuilt worse. Once rebuilt a corvette out of parade leftovers."

### CAST-FIZZLE — Professor Fizzlebloom
**Venue:** **Fizzy Current Power & Reactor**. **Economy fit:** technical counter and research services within Royal Outfitters / specialist station workshop. **Story position:** progressively available power and experimental-engineering advisor.

**Personality:** sparkling soda-pop genius, delighted by dangerous data, anxious about everything except the danger he personally creates. Initially seems to regard the commander as an experiment that keeps returning; develops real affection and starts worrying about the pilot rather than only the test results.

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** glass bubble helmet with soda vapor, fizzy condenser backpack, tiny round spectacles, overfull blueprint roll and asymmetric gloves; lime-yellow highlights without using color as his only identification.

**Planned systems:** reactors, batteries, cooling, power-routing efficiency, limited-use overcharge gear, risky prototype trials and shipyard power diagnosis. Every unstable prototype needs a measurable tradeoff, failure feedback, and a safe alternative; it cannot become unexplained random save destruction.

**Character hooks:** argues with Tilda about engine safety; exchanges practical emergency fixes with Lolly; eccentric experiments may lead to recoverable reactor side missions. Prototype failure risk must be disclosed before purchase.

**Voice and sample lines:** rapid, bubbly, breathless. "Yes, yes, yes, the reactor hum is *supposed* to sound worried." / "Marvelous! Minimal charring. We're learning so much."

### CAST-BRISTLE — Bristle Mint
**Venue:** **Mint Condition Thrusters**. **Economy fit:** Mobile Trader and traveling propulsion/racing specialist. **Story position:** appears around faster transit lanes and courier contracts.

**Personality:** wiry retired peppermint ace, relentless showoff, overdramatic scarf, too many obviously questionable racing stories; underneath the bravado he keeps watch for pilots in genuine trouble.

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** candy-cane flight goggles, long red-white racing scarf, angular mint helmet, low drag air-racer vest and cocky sideways stance; instantly readable from a silhouette.

**Planned systems:** thrusters, wings, maneuver vanes, turn tuning, timed intercept contracts, lightweight ship advice and optional flight challenges. Future sales must change actual acceleration/steering or other verified flight properties.

**Character hooks:** boasts of beating Marshal Muck's patrol fleet in a sanctioned "toll dispute"; teases Gumbo for bolting extra cannons to everything; can point players toward courier routes.

**Voice and sample lines:** fast, jaunty and cocky. "If the bolts aren't singing, you're not accelerating properly." / "You don't need a heavier ship. You need confidence, timing, and maybe slightly fewer collisions."

### CAST-NOUGABELLE — Sister Nougabelle
**Venue:** **The Blessed Bulkhead**. **Economy fit:** Faction Quartermaster / authorized sanctuary and defense outfitter. **Story position:** friendly protected station and Royal relief route contact.

**Personality:** serene nougat nun-engineer; gentle but impossible to intimidate. Offers tea and encouragement without excusing the Sour Armada's cruelty. The quiet emotional anchor between frantic raids.

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** soft-cream habit, gold-edged shield apron, compact repair satchel and calm expression; strong halo-like metal bulkhead shape, not a magical combat claim.

**Planned systems:** shield components, protection, repair packages, emergency recovery resources, field-rescue contracts, and a later limited **Extra Lime** continue-token service if that mechanic is designed and balanced. "Extra Lime" is the in-universe citrus pun for an extra life/continue, **not** an existing game resource.

**Character hooks:** worried about Pip; respects Lolly's discipline; offers sanctuary-related questlines and a non-punitive way back from an equipment softlock. Relief must not replace legitimate mission failure.

**Voice and sample lines:** soft, deliberate, reassuring. "Courage is not the absence of fear, dear. It is the willingness to launch anyway." / "Come in. Sit down. You look like you've been shot at by people with no respect for craftsmanship."

### CAST-GUMBO — Gumbo Gearloose
**Venue:** **Gumbo's Mounts & Mischief**. **Economy fit:** Scrap Bazaar, with working used-mount and utility inventory. **Story position:** enthusiastic tinkerer and source of salvage-side mysteries.

**Personality:** round, cheerful gumball mechanic with a tool belt upon a tool belt; sticky fingers, obsessive curiosity, and surprisingly excellent instincts for unconventional builds. Loves a ship that seems to have "opinions."

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** squat jawbreaker body, magnifying-lens flip goggles, six pockets of bolts, grease-smeared grin and a tool as big as his arm. Shape should stay readable in miniature.

**Planned systems:** genuine gun mounts and hardpoint adapters, cargo hooks, utility sockets, oddball prototypes, secondhand modules and lost-part recovery jobs. Used parts eventually require explicit quality, damage and legal ownership; current shipyard starter stock is **not** purchased from Gumbo.

**Character hooks:** favors Bristle's speed only when he can bolt a cannon onto it; periodically reports missing parts that later become real salvage missions; invents nicknames for the player's current loadout.

**Voice and sample lines:** bright and enthusiastic. "Oh, now *that's* a ship with opinions." / "If you mount one more cannon on this side, it stops being a design choice and becomes a lifestyle."

### CAST-CITRINA — Madame Citrina Peel
**Venue:** **Golden Rind Exchange**. **Economy fit:** Citric Black Market (also a believable interface for ordinary legal trade when inventory rules allow). **Story position:** morally gray civilian contact across sweet and sour borders.

**Personality:** elegant, sly citrus broker, dangerously well connected, not secretly evil by default; an amused and occasionally generous aunt whose ethics depend on context. Her genuine affection can complicate the player’s trust.

**Future portrait/silhouette brief:** ornate segmented citrus-peel coat, narrow gold glasses, hand fan made of rind, trading-ledger cuffs; refined gold/lime accents, sleek silhouette.

**Planned systems:** rotating rare goods, lawful versus stolen salvage exchange, rumors, intel about ambush lanes and hidden stations, optional black-market equipment and mutually intelligible reputation consequences. Rerolls/rotating stock must be seed/time/state controlled and exploit-resistant, not infinite free inventory refreshes.

**Character hooks:** knows why Marshal Muck is hunting certain convoys; may have useful leads involving Senator Sauer without claiming knowledge of the King's true fate; takes an amused interest in Nougabelle's unwillingness to compromise.

**Voice and sample lines:** silky, leisurely, amused. "My dear commander, legality is simply a flavor profile." / "You didn't buy this from me. You acquired it through destiny."

## 3. Two friendly support pilots that can appear during active sorties

These are *characters with actual aircraft and bounded game effects*, not popup icons that claim to help. Planned encounter selection must be reproducible, clear about unlock requirements, capped to prevent stacking exploits and avoid forcing free rescues into every encounter. A support pilot's craft can appear in the side lane and depart after a limited action window.

### CAST-PIP — Pip Parfait
**Ship:** **Cherry Whiz**, a tiny bright-red/cream wing skiff. **Role:** offensive escort and cheerful junior admirer. **Personality:** fearless, earnest, half-trained and far too eager to be heroic. The player should enjoy hearing Pip arrive and worry when Pip takes a dangerous risk.

**Visual brief (future asset):** whipped-cream hair, oversized aviator gloves, cockpit stuffed with good-luck charms and a skiff that visibly oversteers. The support craft's visual language must be distinct from Sour fighters.

**Proposed in-run actions:**
- Timed strafing pass that actually targets enemy health, with limited rounds and visible shots.
- Mark one dangerous bomber, mine-layer or fast courier; the marker gives a readable target, not an artificial automatic victory.
- Short suppressive pass or decoy aggro on smaller enemies; Pip can occasionally intercept an incoming shot if the encounter has a clear cooldown and visual cue.
- Mission unlock path: defend early Royal caravans, save a trainee squadron or build friendship through a limited quest, then qualify for seeded support events.

**Risk and rules:** no instant assassination of mandatory bosses; no unlimited ally spawns; if Pip's small skiff must retreat, player is warned but the plot does not randomly erase Pip permanently. Test that support fire awards proper enemy deaths/rewards without duplicating them.

**Sample radio:** "I'm here! I'm here! I'm absolutely underqualified, but I'm here!" / "You tag 'em, I pop 'em!"

### CAST-LOLLY — Lolly Wren
**Ship:** **Soft Landing**, a utilitarian caramel-colored rescue tug. **Role:** salvage and survival support. **Personality:** older, steadfast, understated and deeply competent; a little weary of reckless pilots but always brings glue.

**Visual brief (future asset):** high-visibility rescue stripes, rescue hook and tow cable, exterior supply bins, cockpit crowded with patched medical kits.

**Proposed in-run actions:**
- Short tractor collection pass for loose, *real* salvage pickups (awards only what it truly retrieves).
- Drop a visible shield recharge or limited hull repair supply at a reachable point.
- Guide a safe lane through a bomb pattern or briefly clear nearby mine hazards when her tool is equipped.
- Optional emergency **Extra Lime** token/continue if later built as a limited resource with explicit cost, use, and UI; cannot silently revive the player or override chosen no-continue modes.
- Unlock path: aid a medical convoy / return rescued cargo, then increase support-event eligibility.

**Risk and rules:** rescue effects cannot fully nullify harsh missions, money sinks, boss mechanics, or an unwinnable ship; salvage pickup IDs must not be counted twice if Lolly's tractor and the player contact the same object in a tick.

**Sample radio:** "Well, you're not dead, and I brought glue. That's a strong start." / "Try not to explode before I finish helping you."

### Ally encounter grammar (planned, not tuned yet)

A future event director may select *none*, Pip, or Lolly after unlocking them. Use deterministic mission seed and progress/reputation plus a meaningful per-run cooldown/limit; store consumed rescue/continue effects in save-safe state. Allies must have clear arrival/exit silhouettes, audible/captioned cues, a reason to help, and a target selection that respects boss and mission completion invariants.

Enemy projectiles and allied projectiles require team identity; losing an ally must not subtract earned rewards or trigger arbitrary player defeat. All help must be visibly observed on-device and included in 3DoF comfort/performance profiling.

## 4. Recurring Sour troublemakers

These are **authored identifiable recurring antagonists**, not merely aliases of generic Bomber/Interceptor/Ace. Their special systems require actual gameplay and an identifiable counter. They may appear as seeded sortie complications, contracts, or story beats; no recurring enemy is guaranteed to spawn in a mission before its encounter code and assets exist.

### CAST-BLORT — Blort Blister, sour grenadier
**Role:** ranged hazard-throwing nuisance. A squat, round, overstuffed bomber pilot who cackles while juggling fizz-bombs. The craft's cargo pod rattles before a throw.

**Planned attack:** visibly lob bombs down a predictable arc; impact becomes a short-lived area denial patch or bouncing projectile. Bombs must have telegraph, maximum lifespan, collision and cleanup; player can sidestep or shoot them when counterplay is unlocked. Especially troublesome in salvage recovery.

**Sample taunt:** "Catch! Oh, no, don't catch. Actually—YES, CATCH!"

### CAST-NIB — Nib Noxious, sour trapper
**Role:** battlefield mine-layer and obstruction specialist. Narrow jagged ship, smug tactical charts, thoroughly inflated self-esteem.

**Planned attack:** drift across a lane laying a **bounded** trail of sticky mines that impede movement, contest salvage lanes or detonate if crossed. Player can clear mines, evade or use countermeasure tools; older mines time out. High relevance to Captain Cringe's traps.

**Sample taunt:** "The lane is perfectly safe. Your route through it is the problem."

### CAST-LIMEWIRE — Lady Limewire, interference duelist
**Role:** recurring rival mini-elite. Elegant lime-razor craft and crackling citrus interference equipment, with theatrical precision and personal barbs.

**Planned attack:** high-speed flanking strike and a short, well-telegraphed shield/firing disruption **when the player has an understandable counter**. Escaping rival may be recorded for a later authored rematch; she cannot arbitrarily bypass boss requirements or keep an impossible permanent debuff.

**Sample taunt:** "Try to keep up, darling. I'd hate to win without an audience."

### CAST-BIFF — Biff Crumbclank
**Role:** first half of an argumentative two-ship demolition act. Round biscuit armor, oversized bomb rack; always throws *too early* and insists the timing was perfect.

**Planned attack:** first wave of paired staggered bomb drops. His recognizable call tells the player when Baff's follow-up is coming.

**Sample taunt:** "NOW! I mean—oh. I meant now later!"

### CAST-BAFF — Baff Crumbclank
**Role:** second half of the double-act, equally annoying and obsessed with procedure. Crooked wafer stabilizers and a clipboard lashed to the cockpit.

**Planned attack:** secondary patterned volley following Biff, forming a readable cross-lane hazard. Pair bonuses are paid **once** only if both ships are genuinely defeated before escape.

**Sample taunt:** "Biff! That's not the sequence! The SEQUENCE!"

### CAST-MUCK — Marshal Muck, Sour toll enforcer
**Role:** pseudo-lawman and extortionary patrol mini-boss. Bulky armored patrol craft with absurd badges and a public-address horn, always issuing invented fines.

**Planned attack:** "transit inspection" blockade, escort summons, slowdown projectiles and opportunistic salvage collection. His crew must visibly steal/retrieve a physical pickup before the economy ledger removes it; never silently dock credits from the player's saved wallet.

**Sample taunt:** "Under Article Nine of the Sour Transit Code, you've been fined for excessive sweetness!"

## 5. Relationships and narrative placement

| Relationship | Planned payoff |
|---|---|
| Tilda ↔ Fizzlebloom | Safety arguments frame structure-versus-overcharge loadout tradeoffs. |
| Tilda ↔ Pip | Tilda's protectiveness reinforces the player's emotional stake in Pip's calls. |
| Fizzlebloom ↔ Lolly | Research optimism vs. pragmatic field repair; salvage/repair mission opportunities. |
| Bristle ↔ Gumbo | Fast handling versus ridiculous cannon configurations; loadout jokes and technical advice. |
| Nougabelle ↔ Lolly / Pip | Rescue network and relief convoys justify support unlocks. |
| Citrina ↔ Nougabelle | Ethical friction over black-market goods, without equating sour civilians with evil. |
| Citrina ↔ Marshal Muck | Information and extortion pressure lead to convoy interception contracts. |
| Biff ↔ Baff | Comedic dialog doubles as timing telegraph for twin bomb patterns. |
| Lady Limewire ↔ player | Escapes and rematches turn mechanical pressure into rivalry across regions. |

**Regional suggestion, subject to authored campaign design:** Sally Scrunch's blockades introduce Tilda, Nougabelle, Pip and Blort; Captain Cringe's deception lanes emphasize Nib, Bristle and Limewire; Pete Pucker's corrosive sectors bring Fizzlebloom, Lolly and the Crumbclanks; Senator Sauer's political occupation expands Citrina and Marshal Muck. A character may recur outside that chapter; do not confine friends to a single boss level.

The Regent and King's fate remain unresolved until S16. Characters may share rumors, not definitive answers that pre-empt the authored finale.
